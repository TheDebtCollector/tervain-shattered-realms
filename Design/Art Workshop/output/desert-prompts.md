# Desert starter set

Seven 24 × 24 RGBA assets with one editable layer each. Cactus and dry bush have transparent backgrounds. Generated with built-in imagegen and reduced to 24 × 24 using nearest-neighbor sampling and fixed palettes.

## sand

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: retro pixel-art desert sand terrain tile matching simple 24x24 dirt and stone game tiles. One square sand texture filling image edge to edge on exact logical 24x24 pixel grid enlarged. Warm golden beige sand with sparse short soft ochre ripple clusters and a few pale cream grains. Restrained 6-color earthy palette. Low detail readable at 24x24, chunky flat solid square pixels. Flat orthographic texture, no dunes landscape, no objects, no grass, no border, no perspective, no text, no antialiasing, no gradients.

Low detail readable at 24x24, solid square pixels, restrained palette. Cactus almost fills the 24x24 square with a narrow transparent margin, base at bottom. Exact logical 24 by 24 pixel grid enlarged.
