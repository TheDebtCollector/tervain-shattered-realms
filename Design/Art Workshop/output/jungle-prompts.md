# Jungle starter set

Seven editable 24 × 24 RGBA assets. Jungle wood is a full square wood-grain tile. Fern and vine have transparent backgrounds. Artwork generated with built-in imagegen, converted to 24 × 24 with fixed palettes.

## jungle-soil-block

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: retro pixel-art jungle terrain tile matching a simple 24x24 dirt, stone, wood, desert and tundra tileset. Square rich dark reddish-brown jungle soil texture with sparse chunky warm earth grains and a few tiny olive moss patches. Entire square filled edge to edge, subdued 7-color earthy palette. Exact logical 24 by 24 pixel grid enlarged. Low detail, chunky flat solid square pixels readable at 24x24. Flat orthographic material texture, no scenery, no perspective, no cube, no border, no text, no antialiasing, no gradients.
