# Swamp starter set

Seven editable 24 × 24 RGBA assets. Wood is a full square grain texture. Reeds and mushroom have transparent backgrounds. Artwork generated with built-in imagegen and reduced to 24 × 24 with fixed palettes.

## swamp-mud-block

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: retro pixel-art swamp terrain tile matching a simple 24x24 dirt, stone, wood and jungle tileset. One square wet swamp mud texture filling image edge to edge. Dark earthy brown and olive-brown mud in broad chunky irregular clumps, sparse subdued gray-green wet patches, 7 flat earthy colors. No water surface, no grass. Exact logical 24 by 24 pixel grid enlarged to fill square image. Low detail, chunky flat solid square pixels readable at 24x24. Flat orthographic material texture. No landscape, no perspective, no cube, no border, no text, no antialiasing, no gradients.
