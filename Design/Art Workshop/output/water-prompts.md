# Water tiles for all current biomes

Five editable static 24 × 24 RGBA tiles, one layer each. Artwork generated with built-in imagegen and reduced to 24 × 24 with fixed biome palettes.

## temperate-water

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: retro pixel-art water terrain tile matching a simple 24x24 dirt stone wood desert tundra jungle swamp game tileset. One square liquid water surface texture fills image edge to edge. Clear medium blue freshwater, sparse short horizontal pale blue ripple clusters, subtle darker blue broad patches, restrained 6-color blue palette. Exact logical 24 by 24 pixel grid enlarged to fill square image. Low detail, chunky solid square pixels readable at 24x24, flat colors. A few calm gentle ripples, low contrast, no large waves, no foam borders. Flat orthographic texture, no shoreline, no land, no scenery, no objects, no perspective, no border, no text, no antialiasing or gradients.
